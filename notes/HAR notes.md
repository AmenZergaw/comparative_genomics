
HARs are binding sites for transcription factors.

HAR mutation wouldn't change the protein directly, just how much a gene is activated. 

HARs are enhancers not promoters.

Positive selection: a mutation causes a favorable change that aids survival and it's passed on. 

GC-Biased gene conversion: DNA repair glitch during recombination causing A/T to be substituted by G/C

Naturally DNA tends to undergo deamination from C to T, so a persistent GC rich region indicates that a gBGC could be happening. Measuring GC content can help identify this. 

A bias ratio helps identify the directionality of these mutations. weak to strong / strong to weak ratio filters real selection from dna repair glicthes and helps avoid false positives of what could've been driving human evoltion and brain development.
